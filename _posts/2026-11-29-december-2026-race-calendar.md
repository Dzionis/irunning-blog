December is the last big month of the racing year, and one weekend in particular is packed: on **Sunday 6 December** Valencia, Shanghai, Fukuoka, Singapore and CIM all run on the same day. After that come Honolulu, Dallas and Abu Dhabi, then Taipei and Guangzhou, and the year ends with the New Year's Eve 10Ks in Madrid and São Paulo.

Here's the whole month in one place, weekend by weekend, with the marathons, half marathons, 10Ks and 5Ks worth knowing about. Dates are checked against the official race websites as of late November. Where we could only find a date in race listings, it says so.

## Weekend 1: 4–6 December, the busiest Sunday of the year

### The big five marathons on Sunday 6 December

- **Valencia Marathon**, Spain. 35,000 runners, World Athletics Platinum Label and the fastest big-city marathon of recent years. The men's course record is 2:01:48, and this year's organisers have named the women's world record as a target. Entry was by ballot and is full. Our [course guide]({% post_url 2026-11-14-valencia-marathon-course-guide %}), [pacing guide]({% post_url 2026-11-19-how-to-pace-valencia-marathon %}) and [race-week checklist]({% post_url 2026-11-28-valencia-marathon-race-week-checklist %}) cover everything for runners.
- **Shanghai Marathon**, China. The 30th edition, about 30,000 runners, starting at 07:00 on the Bund. This is the race's second and final assessment to join the World Marathon Majors. If it passes, Shanghai becomes the ninth Major in 2027.
- **Fukuoka International Marathon**, Japan. An invitation-only elite men's race of a few hundred runners, starting at 12:10. Small, but serious: it's a selection race for Japan's team at the 2027 World Championships, and the course record is 2:05:16.
- **BYD Singapore International Marathon**. The race formerly known as the Standard Chartered Singapore Marathon, with a new title sponsor this year. 55,000+ runners over three days: the 10K on Friday, the half on Saturday and the marathon on Sunday. Entries closed on 31 October.
- **California International Marathon (CIM)**, Folsom to Sacramento. The classic American PR and Boston-qualifier course: point to point and net downhill. This year it's also the USATF Marathon Championships. Sold out, with about 8,000 finishers last year.

## Weekend 2: 12–13 December

### The big three

- **Honolulu Marathon**, Sun 13 Dec, **05:00**. One of the biggest marathons in the US, with about 29,000 marathon entrants last year and no time limit at all: every finisher gets a time, however long it takes. Warm, humid, and a climb round Diamond Head. Entry is still open; the last fee tier ends on 3 December.
- **BMW Dallas Marathon**, Sun 13 Dec, 08:00. Marathon, half and 50K round White Rock Lake, with the 5K and 10K on Saturday. 19,000+ runners on the Sunday, and the Sunday races are sold out.
- **ADNOC Abu Dhabi Marathon**, Sat 12 Dec. Around 40,000 people expected across the marathon, relay, 10K and 5K, on a flat and fast course along the Corniche. Still open.

### 10Ks and 5Ks

- **Zürcher Silvesterlauf**, Sun 13 Dec. The 50th edition of Zürich's old-town race, with about 24,000 runners over distances from 1.6 to 10 km.
- **Hot Chocolate Run** Louisville (Sat 12) and Tampa (Sun 13).

## Weekend 3: 19–20 December

- **Taipei Marathon**, Sun 20 Dec. Marathon at 06:30 and half at 07:00, World Athletics Gold Label, with tens of thousands of runners through the city centre.
- **Guangzhou Marathon**, Sun 20 Dec. 30,000 places, marathon and half, closed.
- **Tata Steel World 25K Kolkata**, Sun 20 Dec. An elite 25K, a rare distance, and a 10K for everyone else.
- **Maratona di Pisa**, Sun 20 Dec, finishing by the Leaning Tower; still open.
- **São Silvestre do Porto**, Sun 20 Dec, 10K on the Avenida dos Aliados.
- **Irving Frost Marathon** (Sat 19) and **Holly Jolly Marathon** in Camarillo, California (Sun 20): small, local and festive. Dates from listings.

## Christmas week

- **Christmas Day parkrun**, Fri 25 Dec. Hundreds of parkruns put on an extra event on Christmas morning; each one decides for itself, so check your local event's page. New Year's Day is a Friday too, and the old trick of running two parkruns on 1 January no longer counts twice.
- **Corrida de Houilles**, Sun 27 Dec, near Paris. One of the fastest 10Ks in Europe, with an elite race at 17:00 under the lights. 5,000+ runners; sold out.

## New Year's Eve, Thursday 31 December

- **San Silvestre Vallecana**, Madrid. 10K, 42,000 runners in waves through the afternoon and an international elite race in the evening. The course is net downhill, so times don't count as records. Still open.
- **São Silvestre**, São Paulo. The 101st edition of Brazil's famous 15K up and down Avenida Paulista, with 55,000 places.
- **BOclassic**, Bolzano, Italy. Elite 10K for men and 5K for women on a short old-town loop, live on Italian TV.
- **Cursa dels Nassos**, Barcelona. A flat 10K and 5K along the seafront.
- **Silvesterläufe** across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, from Trier to Peuerbach.
