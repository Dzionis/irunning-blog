iRunning 1.1.1 is rolling out on the App Store, and it's built around one idea: your running story shouldn't be scattered across old apps and abandoned watches.

## Import your runs

Your running history, finally in one place:

- **Straight from Apple Health.** Every run you've ever recorded on your iPhone or Apple Watch — with any app — can now be pulled into iRunning in a couple of taps.
- **GPX and FIT files.** Coming from another platform or a Garmin? Export your files and bring them in. Routes, times, and heart rate come along for the ride.
- **The full treatment.** Imported runs aren't second-class citizens. They get everything a native run gets: stats, splits, charts, and share cards.

If you've been running for years but only just found iRunning, this update means you don't start from zero. Your streaks, your progress, your best months — they come with you.

## Sharing, leveled up

The share screen grew from a feature into a playground. Version 1.1.1 adds **17 new templates**, including:

- **Transparent overlays** that put your stats directly on your own photo — made for Instagram Stories. Your sunrise, your splits.
- **Heart rate layouts:** your route colored by heart-rate zone, a beat sparkline, zone bars, and zone splits.
- **A clean route silhouette** for the minimalists.
- **A rep ladder** for interval workouts — every rep, stacked and satisfying.
- **A monospace "terminal log" card** for runners who think in data.

The share screen is now fully localized too, so your cards speak your language.

## Fixed

Two long-requested corrections around pauses:

- Pauses no longer skew your pace. Averages now reflect actual running time — coffee stops are officially free.
- GPX and FIT exports handle pauses correctly as well.

## How to get it

Update to iRunning 1.1.1 on the App Store, then look for **Import** in your run history to bring your past runs home. And if you post a run with one of the new templates, we genuinely love seeing them.

Thanks for all the feedback that shaped this release — keep it coming.
