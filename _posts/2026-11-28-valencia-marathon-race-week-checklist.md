Eight days to go. The hard training is done, and for most of the 36,000 runners in the Valencia Marathon the next week is half taper, half travel: flights, a trip to the expo, a lot of rice, and a very easy last few runs along the Turia.

Here's the whole week before the race on **Sunday, 6 December 2026**, in one place. Expo hours and start times are from the 2026 regulations; the runner's guide, published in race week, has the final details on transport and bag drop. Your box and start time are printed on your bib, and those win.

## The expo and your bib

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Feria Valencia, Av. de les Fires, on the north-west edge of the city. Tram line 4 stops at Fira València |
| **Thursday 3 Dec** | 17:00–21:00 |
| **Friday 4 Dec** | 09:00–21:00 |
| **Saturday 5 Dec** | 09:00–19:00 |
| **Sunday** | No bib pick-up on race day |

Bring the QR code from your registration email and photo ID. If someone else has to collect your bib, authorise them in your runner's area on the race website first and give them a copy of your ID; check the runner's guide for the exact rule, because it has differed between the English and Spanish versions of the regulations. Check your timing chip at the chip stand before you leave. It's on the back of the bib, and the bib is the only timing you'll get.

Go on **Thursday evening or Friday morning** if you can. Saturday is the busiest day, and Feria Valencia is a tram ride from the centre; budget two hours for the round trip without shopping. In recent years the bib pack included a Metrovalencia card with a few free trips, which covers the race-morning tram.

## Getting to the start

The start is on Av. del Professor López Piñero, beside the Palau de les Arts in the City of Arts and Sciences, and the finish is a few hundred metres away on the blue walkway. Roads around the course close early, so:

- **Take the tram.** In recent editions tram line 10 started at 06:00 and ran every five minutes from 06:30 to 09:00, stopping at **Amado Granell–Montolivet** right by the start area. Some other tram lines were partly suspended in the morning. Metrovalencia publishes the race-day timetable in race week.
- **Or walk.** If you're staying in Ruzafa, El Carmen or near the Turia, the start is a 20–40 minute walk along the riverbed park. It's a lovely warm-up and you won't queue for anything.
- **Don't count on a taxi.** With the roads closed, they're slow and hard to find.

Aim to be at the start area **an hour before your box goes**. The toilet queues are long at 08:45.

**Bag drop** had to be booked in advance, and it's full. If you have a place, it's on López Piñero next to l'Umbracle, close to both start and finish; one backpack or cabin bag. If you don't, wear old layers you can throw away at the start, and arrange for someone to meet you after the finish with something warm.

## Day by day

| Day | Run | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun 29 Nov** | Last longer run: 60–75 min easy | If you're on a plan, this is the last long one. Easy, nothing to prove |
| **Mon 30** | Rest | Check your box on the bib email. Look at the weather once, then stop |
| **Tue 1 Dec** | 30–40 min easy, with a few minutes at goal pace in the middle | Plan your race: pace per kilometre and your 10 km and halfway targets. The [pacing guide]({% post_url 2026-11-19-how-to-pace-valencia-marathon %}) has the tables |
| **Wed 2** | Rest, or travel | Try on your full race kit. Charge the watch and the headphones |
| **Thu 3** | 20 min easy + **4 × 20-second strides** | Expo opens at 17:00. Start carb-loading at dinner |
| **Fri 4** | Rest, or 15 min very easy | Expo if you haven't been. Walk the last kilometre of the course along the Alameda to the walkway; picture it |
| **Sat 5** | 15–20 min very easy, in the morning | Off your feet by lunchtime. Lay out your kit, pin on the bib, set two alarms |
| **Sun 6** | **Race day** | Breakfast about three hours before your box starts |

The strides on Thursday are the only fast running of the week. Everything else is easy or nothing. If you've just flown in on Friday, swap Friday and Saturday: a gentle 15-minute shake-out after the flight helps your legs more than lying in the hotel.

## Carb-loading in Valencia

From Thursday dinner to Saturday dinner, eat more carbohydrate, not more food: rice, bread, pasta, potatoes, fruit and juice, with less fat and fibre than usual. Valencia makes this easy and slightly dangerous:

- **Paella is fine, in moderation.** Rice is exactly what you want; a heavy, oily paella with a lot of seafood the night before, less so. Have it for lunch on Friday, not dinner on Saturday. The race has held a pre-race paella party on Saturday in recent years if you want the experience in a runner-friendly version.
- **Spanish dinner is late.** Many restaurants don't open their kitchens until 20:30 or 21:00. On Saturday, eat early: a hotel restaurant, a simple pasta place, or your own food. Bed by 22:00.
- **Watch the horchata and the fried stuff.** Lovely, and both better on Monday.

## Race morning, by box

| Entered time | Start | Be at the start area by |
|---|---|---|
| Under 2:50 | 08:15 | 07:15 |
| 2:50–3:11 | 08:25–08:35 | 07:25–07:35 |
| 3:12–3:31 | 08:45–08:55 | 07:45–07:55 |
| 3:32–3:55 | 09:05–09:15 | 08:05–08:15 |
| 3:56–4:09 | 09:25 | 08:25 |
| 4:10 and over | 09:35 | 08:35 |

A sample morning for a runner in the 09:15 box, staying in the centre: alarm 05:45, breakfast at 06:15 (toast, banana, coffee; what you've practised before long runs), leave at 07:30, tram or walk to the start, bag drop and toilets by 08:30, into the box by 08:50, start 09:15. Build your own version backwards from your start, and add 15 minutes to everything.

Two things specific to Valencia:

- **The time limit is 5 h 30 min, and the km 25 cut-off is at 12:57 on the clock**, whichever box you started in. It only matters if you plan to walk a lot, but if you're in the 09:35 box and planning a run-walk, know it's there.
- **The finish area is long.** After the walkway you collect your medal, then the goodie bag with the finisher shirt, then your bag if you booked one. Allow 10–15 minutes from the line to the exit. Mobile signal near the finish is poor with 30,000 phones in one place, so agree a meeting point and a time with family before the race, outside the runners-only area.

## Your kit list

**To race:** bib (pinned the night before, chip untouched), trained-in shoes and socks, race kit, gels for the whole race (the course has gels only at km 16.2, 25.8 and 34), anti-chafe balm, sunglasses or a cap: the low winter sun on the eastbound avenues is bright.

**For the start:** old layers to throw away (they're collected), a bin bag or poncho if it's cold or wet, a bottle of water, your own breakfast if you start late, a little toilet paper.

**For after:** if you booked bag drop, dry warm clothes, a top with long sleeves, flip-flops or soft shoes, a snack. If you didn't, ask someone to bring them to your meeting point.

## How iRunning helps this week

- **Taper runs, with brakes.** Give your easy runs a pace band whose top end is your easy pace, and the app will tell you to slow down when race-week nerves turn 30 easy minutes into a tempo. If you're on the **Marathon** or **Sub-4 Marathon** plan, this week's sessions are already set.
- **Build your race.** Four distance steps with pace bands from the [pacing guide]({% post_url 2026-11-19-how-to-pace-valencia-marathon %}), or a single 42.2 km step with your goal band. Send it to Apple's Workout app with **Add to Apple Watch**, or run it in the iRunning Watch app.
- **Charge the watch on Saturday**, not Sunday morning. In your box, start the workout early enough for GPS to lock on before you reach the start line.
- **Announce the splits.** In **Settings › Customise › Run screen › What to announce**, choose *By distance* every 1 km with *Avg pace*, *Distance* and *Total time*.
- **After the finish.** Share the run as a photo overlay or a route video from the walkway, and then follow the [first two weeks after a marathon]({% post_url 2026-09-29-after-marathon-recovery-first-two-weeks %}). The [course guide]({% post_url 2026-11-14-valencia-marathon-course-guide %}) has everything about the route itself.
