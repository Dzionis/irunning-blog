## Marathons and halves in the US

### St. Jude Memphis Marathon Weekend: Saturday 5 December

The 25th running, and one of the most moving courses in the country: it goes **through the St. Jude Children's Research Hospital campus**, with patients and families cheering from the side. Over 22,000 people ran across the weekend last year.

- **Marathon and half at 08:00, 10K and 5K at 07:00.** The time limit is 6 hours 30 minutes.
- **All in-person races are sold out.** A virtual option is still open.

### San Antonio Marathon and Half: Sunday 6 December

The second edition of the race that replaced Rock 'n' Roll San Antonio, finishing at Hemisfair downtown. Around 18,000–19,500 people ran last year, and it sold out. There's a 5K on Friday 4 December.

- **Half at about 07:15** (last year's time).
- **Still open** when we checked, but it can close at capacity and there's **no race-day entry**.

### OUC Orlando Half: Saturday 5 December

The **50th running**, finishing at Lake Eola downtown, with the Lake Eola 5K on Sunday. Run both for the 16.2-mile challenge. The half starts at 07:15 and is open; the fee went up on 24 November.

### Rehoboth Seashore Marathon and Half: Saturday 5 December

A flat, coastal course in Delaware with some trail sections and 3,400 runners last year. Both races start at 07:00, and entry is open until 3 December. If you missed CIM and want a flat December marathon, this is a good one.

## 10Ks, 5Ks and the shorter stuff

### Course de l'Escalade, Geneva: 5–6 December

**Switzerland's biggest race**, with more than 59,000 people last year across dozens of categories, from kids' races to the main 7.3 km through the old town. Many runners dress up, especially in the "Marmite" fun run. It commemorates the night in 1602 when Geneva beat back a surprise attack, and the city celebrates all weekend.

### Santa runs

December's festive runs start this weekend:

- **BTR Liverpool Santa Dash**, Sunday 6 December, 09:30. A 5K and a 1K Mini Dash, with thousands of runners in Santa suits through the city centre.
- **Las Vegas Great Santa Run**, Saturday 5 December. 5K and a mile, Santa suit included in the entry. Sources disagree on the start time, so check the race page.

### Hot Chocolate Run

The **Hot Chocolate 15K, 10K and 5K** in **St. Louis** and **Phoenix** on Sunday 6 December. Chocolate fondue at the finish, which is all most people need to know.

### Singapore's night 10K

On Friday 4 December at 20:30, the Standard Chartered 10K kicks off the Singapore weekend, with a 5K at 21:30. Running a 10K in the tropical night is a very different thing from a December 10K anywhere else. More in Friday's [Singapore preview]({% post_url 2026-12-04-singapore-marathon-2026 %}).

## Running your own race this weekend?

If you don't have a race, this is a good weekend to do something that feels like one: a parkrun, a 5K time trial, or the last long run before Christmas. Whatever you do, take it easy on the first kilometre. That's the most common mistake in every race listed above.
