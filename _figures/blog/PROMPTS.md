Lesson from the walking photo: "brisk", "purposeful stride" and "arms pumping" make Gemini draw a jog. Spell out the gait (one foot always on the ground, no airborne phase, heel strike, upright torso, modest arm swing), and ask for a fresh chat with an explicit direction ("both toward the camera") — inside one chat it keeps re-drawing the previous composition.

Lesson from the first round: the overlays are white type with one soft shadow, so story backgrounds must be genuinely dark (dusk, blue hour, deep shade). Bright skies and misty water made the numbers unreadable.

Use generated people only as illustration, never as a named user, testimonial, or "real review".
