Your privacy is important to me. It is Dzionis Brek's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information I may collect from you through my app, iRunning.

I only ask for personal information when I truly need it to provide a service to you. I collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent. I also let you know why I'm collecting it and how it will be used.

I only retain collected information for as long as necessary to provide you with your requested service. What data I store, I'll protect within commercially acceptable means to prevent loss and theft, as well as unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use or modification.

I don't share any personally identifying information publicly or with third-parties, except when required to by law.

## Location Data

iRunning uses your device's GPS to record your runs — distance, pace, and route. Location recording only happens while a workout is active, including in the background if you lock your phone mid-run. Your routes and workout history are stored on your device and are not collected by me or sent to my servers.

## Health Data

With your permission, iRunning reads and writes workout data through Apple Health (HealthKit) — for example workouts, heart rate, and energy burned. Health data is used only to power app features such as run tracking, heart rate zones, and workout history. It stays on your device and in your personal Apple Health store. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties, in line with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

## Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using the app, I may collect anonymous usage analytics (for example, which features are used and how often) to understand how the app is used and improve it. Workout content itself — your routes, heart rate, and run history — is not part of analytics.

The app does use third party services that may collect information used to identify you.

## Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive or store your payment details. Subscription status is managed through Apple and my subscription infrastructure provider (Apphud) using anonymous identifiers.

## Your Choices

You can revoke location and Health access at any time in iOS Settings. The app will keep working, though features that depend on those permissions (GPS tracking, heart rate) will be limited. You can also delete your workout history from within the app at any time.

## Links to Other Sites

My app may link to external sites that are not operated by me. Please be aware that I have no control over the content and practices of these sites, and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Your continued use of the app will be regarded as acceptance of my practices around privacy and personal information. If you have any questions about how I handle user data and personal information, feel free to contact me.

This policy is effective as of 20 August 2026.
