On Sunday morning, 30,000 runners will start a marathon on the Bund in Shanghai, and somewhere among the officials there'll be a team from the World Marathon Majors with clipboards. The **Shanghai Marathon** passed its first assessment last year. If it passes the second one on **Sunday 6 December**, it becomes the **ninth Major** in 2027.

That would make it the first Major in mainland China, and it would finally put a date on the **Nine Star medal**. Here's what's at stake, and what the race itself looks like.

## How a race becomes a Major

The World Marathon Majors doesn't take new members on reputation. A candidate race has to pass the series' assessment at **two consecutive editions**. The full criteria aren't published, but the series describes them as covering the whole runner experience "from the Expo to the race course": the standard of the elite field, operations, safety and medical care, course and logistics.

Shanghai's path so far:

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| October 2024 | Named an AbbottWMM candidate race |
| 30 November 2025 | First assessment race |
| 12 March 2026 | Passed: the series said it "met all the criteria" |
| **6 December 2026** | **Second and final assessment** |
| 2027 | Joins as the ninth Major, if it passes |

No date has been set for the decision. When Cape Town went through the same process this year, the confirmation came a few weeks after the race.

## What it means for runners

**More Majors, harder to collect.** Sydney joined in 2025 and Cape Town is confirmed as the eighth for 2027. If Shanghai passes too, the series has nine races.

**The Nine Star medal.** There won't be a Seven or Eight Star medal. The Six Star medal for the original six stays as it is, and the next one is the Nine Star, introduced once Shanghai is in.

**Does a 2026 finish count?** Not confirmed. Cape Town's 2026 finishers were officially told they'd get a provisional star; the series hasn't said the same about Shanghai's 2026 finishers. Assume your star starts with the 2027 race unless the series says otherwise.

**Getting in will get harder.** This year 381,900 people applied for 30,000 places. Add Six Star chasers from around the world and the ballot odds won't improve. Our [Majors 2027 calendar]({% post_url 2026-11-16-world-marathon-majors-2027-calendar %}) covers how entry works for the other eight.

## The course

Shanghai is a point-to-point course from the **Bund**, the riverside boulevard of 1920s banks and hotels facing Pudong's skyline, to **Shanghai Stadium** in Xujiahui. On the way it passes Nanjing Road, Jing'an Temple, Huaihai Road and Xintiandi, then follows the Huangpu riverside along the West Bund and goes past Longhua Temple.

It's **flat**: about 25 metres of total climb by GPS. Chinese media call it the fastest course in the country, and the records back that up.

## Weather

Early December in Shanghai is cool and damp. Daily averages in race week are around **8–9 °C with about 78% humidity**, and over the past 20 years they've ranged from about 4 °C to 12 °C. At a 07:00 start it'll be on the cool side of that: good marathon weather, if it stays dry.

## The elite race

The 2026 elite field hadn't been announced when we checked. Last year's race set the bar:

- **Brigid Kosgei** won the women's race in **2:16:36**, the fastest women's marathon ever run in China, by more than two minutes. Irine Cheptai (2:18:51) and Tiruye Mesfin (2:20:38) were second and third.
- **Milkesa Mengesha** of Ethiopia won a close men's race in **2:06:25**, two seconds ahead of Dawit Wolde. Feng Peiyou ran 2:08:35, a domestic course record, as the first Chinese man.

The men's course record of 2:05:35 is from 2023. A fast elite field would be one way to impress the assessors.

## Race weekend

- **Expo and bib pick-up:** 3–5 December, 09:30–19:30, at the Shanghai World Expo Exhibition & Convention Center in Pudong. Pick-up slots are booked in advance through the race app.
- **Saturday 5 December:** a standalone 6 km fun run with 15,000 places, the first time it has its own day. Race day itself is the marathon and the wheelchair marathon only.
- **Time limit:** listed as 6 hours 30 minutes by race guides; check your race pack.

## How to watch

Shanghai produces an international TV feed of about three hours focused on the elite races, and last year it aired in eight countries and on streaming services such as FloTrack. The 2026 broadcasters hadn't been announced when we checked, so look at the race's social channels in the days before.

Times to set an alarm for: **23:00 on Saturday in the UK**, **18:00 on Saturday in New York**. In Europe that also means you can watch Shanghai on Saturday night and wake up for Valencia, which starts at 08:15 Spanish time on Sunday.
