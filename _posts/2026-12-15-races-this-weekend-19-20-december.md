The last weekend before Christmas is the end of the road-racing year for most people: after this, it's Christmas Day parkrun and the New Year's Eve 10Ks. Asia has the big races this weekend, with **Taipei** (our preview is out tomorrow), **Guangzhou** and an unusual elite race in **Kolkata**. Here's the full list.

## Asia

### Guangzhou Marathon: Sunday 20 December

One of China's big city marathons, with **30,000 places** in the marathon and half, and a World Athletics Gold Label according to race listings. Entry was by ballot and is closed. With Shanghai assessed for the World Marathon Majors two weeks earlier, it's a good weekend to see how deep Chinese road running has become.

### Tata Steel World 25K Kolkata: Sunday 20 December

A rare distance for an elite road race: **25 kilometres**. Kolkata's World 25K was a Gold Label race last year, with Olympic champion Joshua Cheptegei headlining and a bonus on offer for a world record at the distance. There's an Open 10K and fun runs for everyone else. The elite entries for Indian athletes closed in early November.

### Taipei Marathon: Sunday 20 December

Marathon at 06:30, half at 07:00, about 28,000 runners. Our full preview is out tomorrow.

## US

- **Irving Frost Marathon**, Irving, Texas: Saturday 19.
- **Texas Double**, Dallas: two races over Saturday 19 and Sunday 20, for people who want both.
- **Holly Jolly Marathon**, Camarillo, California: Sunday 20.
- **Santa Hustle Orlando** (half, 10K and 5K), **Charlotte Santa Run** (8K and 5K) and **Palm Springs Santa Run** (5K): Saturday 19.
- **Santa Hustle Arizona**, Glendale: Sunday 20.

These are small, local and festive, and listed on aggregator sites only, so check the organiser's page for times.

## The week after: Christmas

- **Christmas Day parkrun**, Friday 25 December. Hundreds of UK parkruns put on an extra Christmas morning run. Our guide on Saturday covers how to find one.
- **Corrida de Houilles**, near Paris, Sunday 27 December: one of Europe's fastest 10Ks, with an elite race under the lights at 17:00. Sold out.
- **New Year's Eve**: San Silvestre Vallecana in Madrid and São Silvestre in São Paulo, both on Thursday 31 December. We'll preview both after Christmas.

## If your season is over

For most runners this is a good week to take it easy: a couple of easy runs, a long walk, and a plan for January. If you're training for a spring marathon, Christmas falls in the middle of your base phase. Our post on [marathon training over the holidays]({% post_url 2026-12-18-marathon-training-over-the-holidays %}) on Friday covers what to keep and what to let go.

And if you'd like a short, fun session to keep you sharp over the holidays, the [10-20-30 workout]({% post_url 2026-12-17-10-20-30-workout-running %}) on Thursday takes 12 minutes of hard running.
