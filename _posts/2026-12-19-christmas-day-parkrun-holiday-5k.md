For a lot of runners, the best part of Christmas morning happens at nine o'clock in a muddy park, in a Santa hat, before anyone has opened a present. **Christmas Day parkrun** has become a tradition in the UK, and this year Christmas falls on a **Friday**, so it's an extra run on top of the usual Saturday.

Here's how to find one, what's changed for New Year's Day, and what to run if you're not in the UK.

## The dates

| Day | Date | parkrun? |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas Day | **Fri 25 Dec** | Extra parkrun day, in the UK and some other countries |
| Boxing Day | Sat 26 Dec | The usual Saturday parkrun |
| New Year's Day | **Fri 1 Jan** | Extra parkrun day, in every parkrun country |
| | Sat 2 Jan | The usual Saturday parkrun |

So if you want, that's four parkruns in nine days.

## How to find a Christmas Day parkrun

Not every parkrun puts on a Christmas Day event. **Each event team decides** whether it can find enough volunteers, and some run on both Christmas Day and New Year's Day, some on one, some on neither. By mid-November several hundred UK events had said yes to Christmas Day, slightly more to New Year's Day, and many hadn't decided yet.

To find one near you:

1. Go to parkrun's **special events page** for your country ("additional parkrun days"). Each event has a **tick** (running), a **cross** (not running) or a **blank** (not decided yet).
2. Check the event's own **news page** for the start time. It's usually 09:00 in England and Wales and 09:30 in Scotland and Northern Ireland, but some events change it on extra days.
3. Check the event's social media and the **cancellations page** on the evening before and on the morning. Frost and ice cancel more December parkruns than anything else.

**Don't forget your barcode.** No barcode, no time, even at Christmas.

## The New Year's Day double is gone

For years, the New Year's Day tradition was the **double**: run one parkrun at 09:00, drive to a neighbouring one with a later start, and get two results on the same day. That's over. parkrun's rules now say **only one result can be recorded on each additional parkrun day**, so the second run won't count.

You can still run two, of course. Only one goes on your record.

## Junior parkrun

Junior parkrun (2 km, for ages 4–14) usually runs on Sunday mornings. In the mid-November check, most junior events weren't planning to run on Christmas Day or New Year's Day; some hold their own festive Sunday runs instead. Check your local junior event's page.

## Outside the UK

- **US:** parkrun USA's extra day is **Thanksgiving**, not Christmas, so there's no Christmas Day parkrun. There is one on New Year's Day.
- **Ireland and Australia** have held Christmas Day parkruns in past years. Check your country's special events page for 2026.
- **New Year's Day** is an extra parkrun day in every country.

## Christmas Day runs that aren't parkrun

- **GOAL Mile, Ireland**: a one-mile charity run or walk on Christmas morning at athletics tracks around Ireland, raising money for GOAL. It's Ireland's best-known Christmas Day run.
- **CRC Christmas Day 5K, Fort Worth, Texas**: Trinity Park, 5K at 08:15 and a mile at 07:45, with a 10K for the first time this year.
- **Jingle All the Way, Long Beach, California**: 5K and 10K at 08:00, and a half at 07:00, on Christmas morning.

In the UK and Australia we didn't find any well-known Christmas Day 5Ks or 10Ks besides parkrun. Christmas Day parkrun is the race.

## Boxing Day

- **A Merry Christmas Run**: 5K and 10K events on Saturday 26 December in Chicago (Evanston), Seattle, Sacramento, Memphis and Atlanta.
- **Boxing Day Challenge**, Egham, UK: laps of a flat Thames path, from 10K up to the marathon, starting at 07:00.
- And the usual **Saturday parkrun**, of course.

## How to enjoy a Christmas parkrun

- **Wear the hat.** Or the jumper, or the antlers. Nobody's racing for a PB on Christmas Day. Well, a few people are, and they're fine with being overtaken by a reindeer.
- **Volunteer, if you can.** Christmas events only happen because volunteers give up their morning. If your local event is short of marshals, that's a lovely thing to do on 25 December.
- **Bring something warm for afterwards.** Many events end with mince pies or a flask of coffee. You'll be cold once you stop.
- **Watch for ice.** Paths in parks are rarely gritted. Shorter strides and a bit more patience on corners. Our [winter running guide]({% post_url 2026-11-05-winter-running-guide %}) has more.

## Training plan over Christmas?

If you're in a spring marathon or half marathon plan, a parkrun on Christmas Day can be your quality session for the week: run it at tempo effort, comfortably hard rather than flat-out. Or jog it with family and call it an easy run. Our [holiday training guide]({% post_url 2026-12-18-marathon-training-over-the-holidays %}) covers how to keep a plan on track over the holidays.

In iRunning, if you run a parkrun with your watch's Workout app instead of iRunning, the run comes in from Apple Health and can count towards that day's plan session. And if you want to remember it, there's a share card waiting at the end.

Merry Christmas, and see you at the start line.
