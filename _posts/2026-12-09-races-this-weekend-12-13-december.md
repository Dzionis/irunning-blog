The second weekend of December has three headliners, and each has its own preview on this blog: [Honolulu]({% post_url 2026-12-08-honolulu-marathon-2026 %}) and Dallas on Sunday, and Abu Dhabi on Saturday. ## Marathons and halves in the US

The South in mid-December is where American runners go for a late-season PR. Mild weather, flat roads and BQ-certified courses: these five are all on the same weekend.

| Race | Day | Where | Start | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kiawah Island Marathon & Half** | Sat 12 | Kiawah Island, SC | 08:00 | Flat island course; both races BQ-certified; Jingle Shells 5K on Friday; closes at capacity |
| **Jacksonville Marathon** | Sat 12 | Jacksonville, FL | 07:00 | Flat, BQ course from the Bolles School; marathon, half and 5K |
| **Rocket City Marathon** | Sun 13 | Huntsville, AL | 07:00 | 6-hour limit with a 06:00 early start; about 200 marathon places and a handful of 10K places left; 10K and 5K on Saturday |
| **Palm Beaches Marathon** | Sun 13 | West Palm Beach, FL | – | Flat waterfront course; BQ; prize money for the first time; 5K and 10K on Saturday |
| **Tucson Marathon** | Sun 13 | Tucson, AZ | 07:15 | Net-downhill desert course, BQ-popular; about 3,000 runners; one listing says Saturday, so check the date |

If you were going to run CIM and didn't get in, or ran it and want another go, these are the races to look at. Entry for most closes the day before.

### Hot Chocolate Run

The **Hot Chocolate 15K, 10K and 5K** comes to **Louisville** on Saturday 12 December and **Tampa** on Sunday 13 December.

### Santa runs in the US

- **San Diego Santa Run** (5K), **Santa Run Columbia** (South Carolina) and **Run Santa Run 5K** in Madison, Wisconsin: Saturday 12.
- **Silicon Valley Santa Run** in San Jose (its 15th edition), the **Great Santa Run 5K** in Leawood, Kansas, and **Santa Hustle** races in Roseville, California, Round Rock and San Antonio, Texas: Sunday 13.

Most smaller races are listed on aggregator sites only; check the organiser's page before you go.

## Europe

### Zürcher Silvesterlauf: Sunday 13 December

The **50th edition** of Zürich's December city race, with about 24,000 runners over distances from 1.6 km for kids up to 10 km on a loop of the old town. Despite the name ("New Year's Eve race"), it's held in mid-December. This year the route has changed because of roadworks.

### Marathons

- **Maratona di Reggio Emilia**, Italy, Sunday 13: marathon and half from Piazza della Vittoria. Entries close on 4 December (dates from listings).
- **São Silvestre de Lisboa**, Portugal: a 10K from Praça Duque de Saldanha, listed for Sunday 13, although one listing says 27 December. Check before booking.

### UK Santa runs

- **Santa in the City**, London: evening runs in Paternoster Square, 8–10 December, at 19:15. Santa suit included, with a fundraising minimum.
- **Santa Run North**, Durham (2.5K and 5K): Saturday 12.
- **Norwich Santa Dash** (5K at 11:00): Sunday 13.

## Latin America

**Maratón de Monterrey**, Mexico, Sunday 13 December, 06:45 from Parque Fundidora. One of Mexico's fastest marathons, with 8,000–10,000 runners. It's listed as sold out (date from listings).
