Thanksgiving takes the spotlight in the US this week, but the weekend after is busy too: three marathons on three continents, and a run of small, festive 5Ks and 10Ks that start the Christmas racing season. Here's what's on, by distance.

## Marathons

### Firenze Marathon, Florence: Sunday 29 November, 08:30

The 42nd Firenze Marathon starts and finishes on **Piazza del Duomo**, and the course in between is a tour of the city: Cascine Park, the Arno embankments, the Oltrarno, **Ponte Vecchio** and Santa Croce. It's flat, and cool late-November weather makes it a popular PB race.

- **Sold out**, with a waiting list since September. Last year 13,200 people entered the marathon, half of them from abroad, and 10,195 finished: the first time over 10,000.
- **Start:** 08:30 CET (07:30 in the UK). There's also a 10K on Sunday and a 3 × 7 km charity relay on Saturday.
- **Course records:** 2:08:40 for men (James Kutto, 2006) and 2:24:17 for women (Lonah Chemtai Salpeter, 2018). Last year Badr Jaafari of Italy won on his marathon debut in 2:09:58.

### Seattle Marathon and Half: Sunday 29 November

The oldest marathon in the Pacific Northwest, and a hilly one: the organisers call it "rolling", and runners tend to use stronger words. It starts near Seattle Center and finishes on the waterfront at Pier 66.

- **Marathon at 07:00, half at 08:30** (Pacific time), in short waves. Some older pages show the 2025 times the other way round; the official site has 07:00 for the full.
- **Entry:** open until Thanksgiving Day, 26 November. There's a Kids Marathon on Saturday morning.
- About 4,600 people finished the two races last year.

If Seattle is your first winter marathon, plan for the climbs and for cold rain: hold back on the hills in the first half and you'll pass a lot of people in the second.

### Amazing Thailand Marathon Bangkok: Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November

The biggest race of the weekend by far: **71,435 runners** are registered, about 9,000 of them from abroad. It's the final race of the World Capital Marathon Series, and it has the most unusual start time on this list.

- **Full marathon: Sunday at 02:00** local time from Sanam Luang, beside the Grand Palace. That's 19:00 on Saturday in the UK. Starting in the middle of the night is the only way to finish before Bangkok's heat arrives.
- **Half marathon: Saturday at 04:00** from MBK Center. 10K at 06:00 and a 4.5 km family run at 06:45 on Sunday.
- **The route** crosses the Rama VIII Bridge, runs an out-and-back on an elevated road, and goes through Silom, Sathorn and Ratchadamnoen Avenue, with all roads closed.
- Last year's winners ran 2:14:27 and 2:39:27, with Eliud Kipchoge in the field. No 2026 elite field has been announced.

(Not to be confused with the long-running Bangkok Marathon, which moves to January in 2027.)

## Halves, 10Ks and 5Ks

### UK

- **MoRun**, the Movember charity series: Holyrood Park, Edinburgh (Sat 28), Glasgow Green (Sun 29, 10:00) and Campbell Park, Milton Keynes (Sun 29), with 5K, 10K and half options. Moustaches encouraged.
- **RUN Victoria Park 10K**, London, Sun 29 at 10:00. Flat park laps.
- **Cheddleton 10K**, Staffordshire, Sat 28. Every finisher gets a Christmas pudding.
- **Chase the Christmas Pudding 5K**, Tredegar, Sun 29. A festive family run.

### Spain, France and Italy

- **Firenze Marathon 10K**, Florence, Sun 29, on the same morning as the marathon.
- **Carrera Tecnocasa Vuela**, Madrid (Valdebebas), Sun 29: 5K, 10K and half.
- **Marathon de La Rochelle**, France, Sun 29, with a 10K alongside.

### Australia

- **Vision Australia Santa Fun Run**, Melbourne's Tan Track, Sat 28.
- **Run for the Young**, Lilydale, Victoria, Sun 29: 10K at 08:00, plus a 5K, half and full.
- **Red Ribbon Run**, Albert Park, Melbourne, Sun 29.

### US

After Thursday's turkey trots, the US weekend is quiet: no big 5Ks or 10Ks to speak of. If you ran a trot on Thursday, a relaxed long run or a parkrun on Saturday is the perfect follow-up. The holiday series start the weekend after: Hot Chocolate in St. Louis and Phoenix on 6 December, and the Santa runs from 5 December.

Smaller races are listed on aggregator sites only, so check each organiser's page before you travel. And if you can't find a race near you, a solo 5K time trial on Saturday is a good way to see where you are going into December.

## Watching from home

Bangkok is the one to stay up for if you're in Europe: the marathon starts on Saturday evening UK time and finishes before midnight for the leaders. Florence finishes by mid-morning UK time on Sunday. None of the three has announced a 2026 livestream yet; check each race's social media on the day.
