iRunning 1.2.2 is rolling out on the App Store. It's the biggest update since training plans arrived, and most of it came from the same place as always: your feedback.

## Turn your run into a video

**Share › Video** replays your route as an animated clip, with distance, pace, time and heart rate counting up as the dot moves. Five overlay styles, story or portrait format, a light, dark or satellite map, an optional 3D view, and a camera that follows you. Save it to Photos, or hand it straight to Instagram Stories or TikTok.

It's **free for everyone**. No subscription needed.

## 20 new training plans

The catalog goes from 21 plans to 41. The headline group is **Chase a time**: four plans with one number on them.

- **Sub-30 5K**, **Sub-60 10K**, **Sub-2h Half Marathon**, **Sub-4 Marathon**: fixed goal, fixed paces, reps a touch under goal pace, tempos a touch over, and a time trial at the end.

Two groups are entirely new: **Hiking & rucking** (Rucking: First 8 Weeks, Trek Ready) and **After 50** (First 5K After 50, Strong Runs at 50+). And spread across the others: Japanese Walking, Walk a Half Marathon, First 15K / 10-Miler, Run-Walk Half Marathon, First 50K, Faster Mile, Fitness Race 8 × 1 km, Zone 2 Base Builder, The 30-Minute Runner, 28-Day Streak, Postnatal Return to Running and 12-3-30 Incline Walk.

We've started writing about them: [Sub-30 5K]({% post_url 2026-09-12-sub-30-5k-training-plan %}), [Japanese Walking]({% post_url 2026-09-09-japanese-walking-interval-walk %}) and [Zone 2 Base Builder]({% post_url 2026-09-06-zone-2-running-aerobic-base %}) each have their own guide.

## Dark mode

Long requested, finally here. **Settings › Customise › Appearance** offers Light, Dark or System. The app stays daylight-first by default; switch to Dark or System if you run at night or just prefer it.

## Smarter tracking

- **Indoor or outdoor.** Pick **Indoor** on the workout screen before a treadmill run. The app skips GPS and saves the workout to Apple Health as an indoor run. Time-based intervals, voice cues, heart rate and cadence all work as usual. See [Treadmill Intervals That Don't Feel Like a Hamster Wheel]({% post_url 2026-09-10-treadmill-intervals-indoor-running %}).
- **Runs and walks are told apart automatically**, from your cadence and speed, so each workout lands in Apple Health as the right type even if you started a "run" and walked it.
- **Re-sync with Health.** Edited a run that's already in Apple Health? Open it and tap the "Saved to Health" pill to rewrite it with the latest data. This applies to runs iRunning saved itself; runs imported from Health belong to their original app.

## Export many runs at once

In History, long-press a run, tap **Select**, pick as many as you like, and export them together as FIT or GPX. Two or more runs come as a single ZIP. Batch export is part of iRunning Premium, like the single-run GPX and FIT export.

## Also in this release

- **iCloud sync has its own switch** in Settings › Devices. It's off until you turn it on, and it's part of Premium. If you were syncing before, flip it back on after updating.
- Polish across the run screens, History and plans.

## Fixed

- Pace targets and pace alerts now display correctly in miles.
- A run whose workout you deleted in the Apple Health app can be saved to Health again.

## How to get it

Update to iRunning 1.2.2 on the App Store. Then open a recent run, tap Share, and try **Video**. We'd love to see what you make.
