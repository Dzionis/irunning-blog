Texas's oldest marathon started in 1971 with 82 runners on the shore of **White Rock Lake**. On **Sunday 13 December**, the 55th BMW Dallas Marathon still goes round the lake, along with more than 19,000 people across the marathon, half, 50K and relays. Every Saturday and Sunday race is **sold out**.

This year's route has changed, so even if you've run Dallas before, there's something new. Here's the guide.

The marathon, half, 50K and relays all start together in waves. Your corral is printed on your bib. You can move back a corral, but not forward.

## The 2026 route

The course is a big loop from downtown out to **White Rock Lake** and back. For 2026 it goes out via **Turtle Creek, Avondale, Fitzhugh and Cole**, and it no longer includes the Lakeside Drive and Highland Park section used last year.

| Miles (approx.) | Where |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Downtown and Victory Park |
| 3–5 | Cedar Springs, **Turtle Creek Blvd**, Avondale and Fitzhugh |
| 6–9 | Cole, then **Greenville Avenue** and Lakewood: the main climb |
| 10–18 | **White Rock Lake**: west shore, the north end, the east shore on the lake trail, round to Winsted |
| 19–21 | Gaston, Richmond and Greenville again: the second climb |
| 22–26.2 | Ross Avenue and back downtown, mostly downhill to City Hall Plaza |

The mile markers above are our reading of the official 2026 map, so treat them as approximate.

### Where the hills are

Dallas isn't hilly, but it isn't flat. The official elevation chart runs between about 420 and 600 feet, and there are two climbs worth knowing:

1. **Miles 6–9**, from Monticello up Greenville and Richmond to Lakewood. It's early, so the danger is running it too hard.
2. **Miles 20–21**, back up Gaston and Richmond. Late, short and unwelcome.

The lake loop between them is lower and rolling, and the last five miles are mostly downhill into downtown. That makes Dallas a good course for a patient, even race: easy up the first climb, steady round the lake, and use the downhill finish.

## Aid, pacers and cut-offs

- **17 aid stations**, all with water and Gatorade Endurance Formula. The listed marathon miles are 2.1, 4.2, 5.7, 7.3, 8.9, 9.3, 11.5, 12.5, 14.1, 15.3, 16.7, 18.6, 20, 21.5, 23.2 and 24.6 (one more at 20.5 is for the 50K only). These may still be last year's mile points, so check the final course map.
- **Pace groups** from the Dallas Running Club, at even splits: 3:15, 3:30, 3:45, 4:00, 4:15, 4:30, 4:45, 5:00 and 5:15 for the marathon, and every 10 minutes from 1:30 to 3:00 for the half. Sign up at the DRC booth at the expo.
- **Cut-offs:** the marathon and half split at **mile 7.8** (Richmond & Greenville), which closes at **10:45**. The 50K cut-off is at mile 18.5 at 12:00. Behind the rolling 14:50-per-mile pace, roads reopen.

## The weather: dress for a cold start

The average race-morning temperature is around **40 °F (4 °C)** at 08:00, rising to about 50 °F (10 °C) by early afternoon. Last year was described as "frigid", and some years have started near freezing with wind chill.

Bring a throwaway layer for the corral, gloves you can drop, and something on your ears for the first hour. The race runs rain or shine, and once in its history, in 2013, it was cancelled by an ice storm.

## Is Dallas a Boston qualifier?

Usually yes: the race lists itself as a Boston qualifier under USATF certification TX21063LAB. But because the route changed for 2026, the official 2026 map says the marathon certification is **pending**. If you're chasing a BQ, check that the 2026 course has been certified before race day. It almost certainly will be, but it's worth confirming.

## The race at the front

Dallas doesn't publish a prize purse for the marathon: winners get a watch. That makes it more of a local and regional championship than an international elite race. The race records, 2:12:04 and 2:29:55, both date from 2006 and were set on older courses.

Last year **Steven Fahy**, from Dallas, won the men's race in 2:23:45, and **Alauna Carstens** won the women's in 2:53:50. In the half, Olympian Dakotah Popehn ran a course record of about 1:12.

## Expo and bib pick-up

At the **Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center, Hall A**: Friday 11 December 11:00–18:00 and Saturday 12 December 09:00–18:00. **No race-day pick-up and no proxy pick-up**, "no exceptions". Bib numbers are emailed on the Wednesday before.

## Where to watch

- **Turtle Creek** (miles 3–4), early and pretty.
- **Greenville Avenue**: runners pass twice, around mile 7 and again around mile 21, so one spot gives you two sightings. Last year's big cheer zone was on Greenville near mile 21.
- **Lakewood**, Gaston and Richmond (miles 8–9 and 19–20).
- **White Rock Lake** (miles 11–18), for a calmer view. DART's White Rock and Mockingbird stations are near the course.
- **The finish** at City Hall Plaza.

**On TV**, CBS Texas is the broadcast partner; last year it aired live from 07:00 to 08:04 with a stream on cbsdfw.com. The 2026 schedule and tracking app hadn't been confirmed when we checked.
